# Conversational application surface brief

## Mode and decision

- Mode: Operate, with a dedicated Read state for long-form d'var Torah content
- Direction: Braided Light
- Seed: `8feeff99`
- Composition: source-first workspace derived from the approved Source Loom identity

The signed-in product carries the public promise into a quieter working environment. A branded rail holds conversations and account actions; the primary workspace gives the question composer, curated prompts, and weekly learning clear but unequal weight. Once a question is sent, assistant material becomes a reading surface with explicit source definitions rather than a stack of generic chat bubbles.

## Component grammar

- Rail: persistent desktop navigation, modal overlay on mobile, 44px actions throughout.
- Composer: persistent visible label, restrained rectangular action, draft-safe input behavior.
- Answer: readable assistant surface with `dir="auto"`, locally matched and model-knowledge source labels, actions, and a separate user-message treatment.
- State: explicit skeleton, loading, streaming, timeout, error, empty, disabled, success, and human-referral patterns.
- Dialogs: trapped focus, Escape dismissal, focus restoration, live status, and isolated credit versus sponsorship selectors.
- Reader: wide calm measure, semantic paragraphs, dedication context, and no chat-bubble styling.

## Compatibility commitments

Streaming SSE event names, analytics event names, authentication `postMessage`, payment bodies, denomination values, package IDs, sponsorship tiers, conversation history, text-to-speech, feedback, and referral behavior remain unchanged. Streaming text is painted at most once per animation frame and finalized once; automatic scroll stops when the reader moves away from the bottom.

## Responsive commitments

At 390px the rail is an overlay with one synchronized open state, the composer and response column stay within the viewport, settings become a linear task flow, and dialogs remain keyboard and touch operable. At 200 percent zoom the main path reflows rather than requiring page-level horizontal scrolling.

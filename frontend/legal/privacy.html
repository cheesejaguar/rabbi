Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 1, 2026

This policy explains what rebbe.dev ("we") collects, why, and your rights. We aim to collect the minimum needed to run the Service.

Sensitive data. Your conversations may reveal religious beliefs and, sometimes, information about your health, relationships, or emotional state. Under laws like the GDPR this is "special category" data. We treat it with heightened care, process it only to provide the Service, and you can export or delete it at any time.

What we collect

  • Account information — name and email from our authentication provider (WorkOS).
  • Conversations — the messages you send and the responses generated, so you can revisit them.
  • Profile preferences — e.g. denomination and an optional bio you provide.
  • Payment information — handled by Stripe. We never receive or store full card numbers; we store a Stripe customer reference and purchase records.
  • Usage & device data — pages viewed, sessions, referrer, and coarse device type, for analytics and abuse prevention. IP addresses are used transiently for rate limiting and security.
  • Safety signals — if the Service detects a possible crisis, it records that a review-worthy event occurred so a human can help; this is retained for safety and audit.

How we use it

  • To operate the Service and generate responses.
  • To maintain your history, credits, and preferences.
  • To keep the Service safe, prevent abuse, and comply with law.
  • To improve quality (e.g. reviewing thumbs-down feedback). We do not sell your personal data.

Who we share it with (processors)

  • LLM provider — your message and relevant context are sent to our AI provider to generate a response.
  • Stripe — payment processing.
  • WorkOS — authentication.
  • ElevenLabs — only when you use text-to-speech, the text to be spoken is sent for audio generation.
  • Infrastructure/hosting and error-monitoring providers, under confidentiality obligations.

Retention

Conversations are retained until you delete them or your account. Raw analytics and error logs are retained for a limited period (by default, up to 365 days) and then purged. Payment records are retained as required for tax and accounting.

Your rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to access, correct, export, or delete your personal data, and to object to or restrict certain processing. You can export or delete your data from your account settings, or contact us. Deleting your account removes your conversations, feedback, and profile.

Security

We use encryption in transit, signed session cookies, access controls, and rate limiting. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your data.

Children

The Service is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data.

International transfers

Our providers may process data in the United States and elsewhere. Where required, we rely on appropriate safeguards for such transfers.

Changes

We may update this policy; material changes will be reflected in the date above and, where appropriate, by re-requesting consent.

Contact

Privacy questions or requests: privacy@rebbe.dev.

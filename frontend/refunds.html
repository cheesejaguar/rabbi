Payments and refund requests

Before paying

Review the credit quantity or sponsorship details and total shown in checkout before confirming. Keep the Stripe receipt or transaction reference. Never send full card details in a support request.

Request a review

For duplicate or incorrect charges, failed purchases, or other refund questions, use the private contact instructions and identify the date, amount, and transaction reference. A request does not itself confirm that a refund has been issued.

Credits and statutory rights

Automatic restoration of a credit after a failed chat is separate from a payment refund. Refund eligibility must be assessed against the purchase details and applicable law; nothing on this page excludes mandatory consumer rights.

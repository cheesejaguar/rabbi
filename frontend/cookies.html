Cookies and browser storage

Service storage

Sign-in sessions, guest usage controls, theme preferences, and saved browser state help provide the requested service. Stripe may use storage for payment security when checkout is opened. See the privacy policy for the current storage inventory.

Your controls

Use browser settings to inspect or remove cookies and local storage. Removing them may sign you out or reset preferences. Clearing browser storage does not delete server-side conversations or payment records.

Deletion

See data deletion instructions to request removal of server-side data.

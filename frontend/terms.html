01

What rebbe.dev is

rebbe.dev is an AI guidance companion for Jewish learning, practice, ethics, and personal reflection. It uses AI models to write answers, surfaces a range of views and sources, and recommends human rabbis and professionals when that is wise.

It is guidance, not psak. rebbe.dev is not a rabbi, a posek, a beit din, a therapist, a doctor, or a lawyer, and it does not issue binding halachic rulings. For practical decisions with real stakes, including halachic, medical, legal, financial, or family matters, ask a qualified person who knows you.

02

Not for emergencies

rebbe.dev is not a crisis or emergency service, and nobody monitors conversations in real time to respond to you. If you or someone else is in danger, call your local emergency number (911 in the US and Canada, 999 in the UK, 101 or 100 in Israel). In the US you can call or text 988, the Suicide & Crisis Lifeline. When rebbe.dev detects signs of crisis, it shows resources, but it can miss them.

03

Who can use it

You must be at least 13 years old to use rebbe.dev. If you are 13 to 17, use it only with the permission of a parent or guardian, who agrees to these terms for you. To create an account or buy credits you must be able to form a binding contract where you live, or have a parent or guardian do so.

04

AI output can be wrong

AI answers can be incomplete, outdated, or wrong, including citations. Sources marked "From the library" are matched to passages in rebbe.dev's text library; other references come from the model's general knowledge and should be checked. Do not rely on rebbe.dev as your only source for decisions that matter. Answers are automatically reviewed for safety and may be withdrawn after they appear.

06

Credits and payments

One credit is one answered message. Premium voice uses additional credits, as shown in the app. New accounts receive free credits, and guests get a small number of free messages. Turns where rebbe.dev shows crisis resources are never charged, and if an answer fails to arrive, its credit is returned automatically.

Payments are processed by Stripe. Credits have no cash value, cannot be transferred, and do not expire while the service operates. Prices are set close to cost and may change for future purchases; credits you already bought keep their value. If something went wrong with a purchase, contact us and we will make it right, including a refund of unused credits where appropriate.

D'var Torah sponsorships are voluntary dedications. Dedication text you submit is shown publicly with that week's d'var Torah; please don't include anything private about another person without their permission.

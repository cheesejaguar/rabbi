01

What rebbe.dev is, and what it is not

rebbe.dev is an AI tool for learning about Jewish texts, practice and ideas. Its answers are guidance, not psak (a halachic ruling). The Service is not a rabbi, and nothing it says replaces asking a qualified rabbi you know. For any practical question of halacha, especially one that touches kashrut, Shabbat, family purity, conversion, mourning, marriage or divorce, ask your own rabbi before you act.

02

Perspective

By default, answers come from a progressive Modern Orthodox perspective. If you're signed in, you can choose a different denomination in your settings, and answers will try to reflect it. Jewish communities disagree on many questions. An answer from one perspective doesn't mean other views are wrong or illegitimate.

03

How sources work

Answers may cite sources in two ways, and we label which is which:

  • Locally matched: the passage was found in the texts the Service searches, and the citation points to it.
  • Model knowledge: the reference comes from the AI model's general training, not from a passage we matched. These can be wrong, misquoted or invented.

AI can misread, mistranslate or mistransliterate Hebrew and Aramaic. Check important citations against the original text, for example on Sefaria.

Not for emergencies or professional advice

The Service is not medical, mental-health, legal or financial advice. If you or someone else is in danger, contact local emergency services.

How it works

Listens first

Before answering, the system considers how to respond — whether you're learning, struggling, or simply curious — and adjusts its tone and care accordingly.

Grounded in the sources

Answers draw on a library of Tanakh, Mishnah, Talmud, Midrash, Rambam, Shulchan Aruch, and more — with citations shown so you can check every claim.

The full landscape

Halacha is presented as a living conversation: majority views, minority opinions, and the principles underneath — never a single verdict pretending to be the only one.

Dignity as a rule

Every response passes an ethical check: an answer that shames or harms the person asking is treated as a wrong answer, however technically correct.

What this is — and what it isn't

This is guidance, not psak. rebbe.dev is an AI study companion. It does not issue halachic rulings, and it will regularly point you toward a rabbi who knows you personally for questions that deserve one.

It is not therapy. If you're going through something serious, it will encourage you to speak with a human — a rabbi, a counselor, or a mental-health professional.

It shows its sources. Citations that were checked against the text library are marked as verified, so you can tell what was retrieved from primary sources and what comes from the model's general knowledge.

It rests when you do. On Shabbat and yom tov the site gently notes the day — the conversation will be here after havdalah.

A d'var Torah every week

Each week brings an original teaching on the parsha — and you can sponsor the week's learning in memory or in honor of a loved one, in the tradition of dedicated Torah study.

Simple, honest pricing

Questions people ask

Is this a replacement for my rabbi?

No — and it will tell you so itself. Think of it as a knowledgeable study partner available at 2am: a starting point for deeper exploration, not a final word. For psak, lifecycle decisions, or anything weighty, it consistently recommends a rabbi who knows you.

What perspective does it answer from?

A progressive Modern Orthodox framework, presented pluralistically. You can also set your own denomination in your profile, and the sources and framing adapt accordingly.

Where do the answers come from?

A four-stage reasoning pipeline over a library of Jewish primary texts. Sources are cited with each answer, and citations that match passages retrieved from the library are marked verified.

Is my conversation private?

Conversations are stored to your private account so you can return to them, and are never shared publicly.

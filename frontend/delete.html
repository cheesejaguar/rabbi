Data deletion requests

Account and conversation records

The current application does not offer self-service account-wide deletion. Follow the private contact instructions to request deletion or access. Identify the account through a private channel; do not include passwords or full card numbers.

Scope and verification

Specify whether your request concerns conversations, profile data, or the entire account. Identity verification may be needed. Some transaction, security, or legally required records may need to be retained; see the privacy policy.

Browser and providers

Clearing local storage removes browser copies and preferences but does not remove server records. AI, speech, authentication, and payment providers may retain records under their own policies.
